Get it

  1. Download LaunchOS.zip and unzip it.
  2. VirtualBox: Machine → Add… and pick LaunchOS.vbox.
  3. USB stick (8 GB or more): write LaunchOS.iso with balenaEtcher, or Rufus in DD mode. This erases the stick. Turn off Secure Boot, then start the PC from the stick.
  4. Install: from the USB stick, open Settings → Install LaunchOS and pick a drive (16 GB or more). This erases that drive.

Questions

Is the free edition complete?

Yes. Pro only adds extras on top.

How many PCs can I use Pro on?

One key works on up to 5 PCs. To free a slot from a PC you no longer use, email us.

Does Pro need internet?

Only to unlock and to get Pro updates. Once unlocked, Pro stays unlocked offline. LaunchOS rechecks the key about once a month when online, and never locks anything because you are offline.

What do you collect?

Only the license key and a random PC id when you unlock. Never your name, email or hardware serial. Payments are handled by the store; LaunchOS never sees card details.

What is your refund policy?

All Pro sales are final. If something is wrong with your purchase, email us and we will sort it out. Your legal rights where you live are not affected.

Is it open source?

LaunchOS is built from open-source parts (Linux, Debian, sway, GTK, Wine, Flatpak) and lists them under Settings → About → Open-source credits. Source code for the GPL parts is available on request and through Debian's source packages.
